## Retrospective Findings (End of Sprint)

- **What went well:** the plane had already bounded this read (E-058) and dated it (E-064), which is why it looked finished — the cost was affordable, so nobody asked whether it was necessary. Removing work is cheaper to defend than bounding it: the answer is identical by construction, and the control proves it.
- **Could be improved:** every previous hardening bought correctness at some cost and said so; this one bought nothing but time, and the bench had no metric for "work a surface does not need" until now. A budget makes a read affordable; only a question about necessity makes it free.
- **Actions:**
