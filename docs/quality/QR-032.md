### Code Review
- **Reviewer(s):** Acceptance Auditor, Edge Case Hunter (parallel adversarial layers), plus the guard and the commit gate as mechanical reviewers
- **Review date:** 2026-11-09
- **Comments (summary):**
  - The normalization is **inside** `_doctor_drift`, not in the shared read path: reads stay side-effect free, so the snapshot still carries the expired alert until the next mutation. That is the deliberate boundary — the diagnostic answers "did the representations diverge?" without pretending to repair the board.
  - Only the `alerts` plane is TTL-filtered and only for `kind == "handoff"`. Every other plane is compared exactly; the census proves the difference with a snapshot-only key, a stale session alert (old but not a handoff), a link, and a replay-only key.
  - The TTL is read from `HANDOFF_SIGNAL_TTL_SECONDS`, the same constant the fold uses, rather than a second literal. A future TTL change reaches both sides at once.
  - The bench measures the shipped comparator through the `_DRIFT_TTL_NORMALIZED` marker and keeps the pre-fix shape behind `--broken`, so the control cannot silently become the treatment.
  - **Review finding #1:** the shipped-board contract test (`test_check_methodology_status.py`) pinned "exactly fifteen stories are done". Adding this sprint's story moves it to sixteen; the assertion and its comment are updated in the same change, which is what that test exists to force — the board cannot gain a story without the contract test saying so.
