## Findings

- **Finding 1 (design decision, accepted in review):** a digest-based classification was the obvious first shape and was rejected. It would read STALE every time a later stage appends to the tracked artifact — noise, not signal — which the bench's `rewritten-with-token` and `glob-other-match` fixtures score as false alarms. The plane re-applies the declared predicate (`contains` + `min`) instead, because that is the claim the completion actually made.
- **Finding 2 (single source, resolved in review):** the completion check and the read-time re-derivation initially had two matchers (`engine._check_evidence`'s inline loop and the new module). They are now one function (`evidence.artifact_matches`); the refusal messages are unchanged, which the existing evidence edge-case tests pin.
- **Finding 3 (scope, deliberate):** `command` evidence ("this argv exited 0") is not dated by this plane — it is history rather than a workspace claim. It is recorded as out of scope in E-056's Known Gaps and remains marked in the plan (§10), not silently dropped.
- **Finding 4 (pre-existing, not caused by this change):** `sh scripts/check-plugin.sh` reports the same 11 `UNVERIFIABLE` records (E-024 … E-034) it reported before this work — the documented machine-local key contract (README: "FORGED on a teammate's record is normal"). The message is the F-14 `UNVERIFIABLE` wording introduced with E-053.
