## Experiment: E-202 — The docs overview counts the areas and files it withheld

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Status:** completed
- **Record Kind:** contract
- **Theory:** The orientation digest's docs section caps two lists and said nothing about either. The walk stops after _MAX_DOCS_SUBDIRS (12) areas, and the text render prints only _MAX_LISTED (6) of the largest files. Observed live on 2026-11-22 on a scratch tree with fifteen area directories and twelve large notes: the digest listed area-00 through area-11 and then stopped, and the render printed six sizes, so both read as the complete inventory. That is the one thing this section exists to prevent — its own docstring says it was built because a session burned dozens of manual ls/find/wc probes — and a silent cap sends the reader back to exactly that sweep, believing it already has the answer. The artifact line in the same render has counted its remainder with '… +N' since E-1xx; the areas and the largest files now do the same, and the digest carries subdirs_total so the count comes from the tree, not from the cap.
