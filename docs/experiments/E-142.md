## Experiment: E-142 — The change-radar check measures the radar it claims to

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Status:** completed
- **Record Kind:** contract
- **Theory:** bench_consistency._check_change_radar_contract_matches compares three things that must agree: the subscriptions the skills ship, the radar the runbooks document, and the table the handoff lint declares. Three defects made that comparison weaker than its own docstring claimed, and all three had been recorded in this repository's deferred ledger since the S-001 review: DW-3, the documented channel was a (session|stop) alternation, so a custom channel's segment failed to parse and the bullet looked like it omitted the skill; DW-4, a subscription was attributed by the --watcher token rather than by the skill whose SKILL.md ships it, so any mention of another skill's command entered the radar under that other skill and the tree could satisfy the contract with a line the owning skill never wrote; DW-5, the bullet was the first line carrying the marker phrase, so a table-of-contents mention became the bullet, parsed as an empty claim, and every skill reported as omitted. A contract check is only as strong as its reader: a reader that cannot parse a legitimate value, attributes to the wrong owner, or picks the wrong line reports agreement it never established.
- **Hypothesis:** H-142: "radar_contract_accuracy == 1.0"
- **Measurement Metrics:** radar_contract_accuracy == 1.0
- **Experiment Design:** Inputs: the shipped tree at this commit. Procedure: scripts/bench/bench_radar_contract.py runs five probes — a custom channel is documentable, a marker-only line is not the bullet, a foreign watcher is not charged to the radar, a foreign watcher fails the shipped check, and the shipped tree still satisfies the contract. Control: --broken reimplements the pre-fix reader (fixed (session|stop) channel list, attribution by the --watcher token, first-marker-line pick) and runs the same probes against it; it scored 0.20. Reproducibility: stdlib only, deterministic, offline.
