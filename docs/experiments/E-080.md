## Experiment: E-080 — Unknown analyzed refs are not reported as known mismatches

- **Date:** 29.10.2026
- **Status:** completed
- **Theory:** The finder maps missing analyzed-version metadata to the string `unknown`, then reports `version_match: false` whenever a caller supplies `--version`. A missing observation is therefore represented as a known incompatibility. Workflows may overstate mismatch instead of recognizing that the source version must first be established.
- **Hypothesis:** H-080: "consistency_accuracy >= 0.90"

They must produce an unknown comparison (`version_match: null` and `analyzed_version_ref: unknown`), while known equal and unequal refs remain true and false respectively. The bench control will restore the old behavior and must detect the false mismatch. Reproducibility is local and offline.
