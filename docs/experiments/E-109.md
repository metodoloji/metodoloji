## Experiment: E-109 — A spawn that inherits the host's stdin never starts: every bench hands its child an explicit stdin

- **Date:** 03.11.2026
- **Status:** REJECTED
- **Theory:** E-045 established the invariant and its census: a `subprocess.run([…])` that inherits the parent's stdio handle dies with WinError 6 before the child exists when the gate runs with no stdin of its own, and the remedy is an explicit `stdin=subprocess.DEVNULL` (or `input=`). The census runs on every activation through `bench_consistency._check_every_spawn_hands_stdin`. The deferred-work integration bench added two unprotected spawns this session (fixed at review time), but `scripts/bench/bench_upstream.py` still carries one pre-existing unprotected spawn — the `patch -R` call in its `--broken` control — so the census is red and the suite is not green. The theory under test is that one explicit stdin on that call closes the invariant: the census returns None and the consistency bench reaches 1.0.
- **Hypothesis:** H-109: "consistency_accuracy >= 0.999"

The `--broken` mode seeds one defect per rule family into a copy of the tree and must score below the threshold, proving the bench can fail (rule 7).
