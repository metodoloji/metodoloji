## Experiment: E-191 — The orient digest bounds values without cutting a token in half

E-187 removed exactly this from the three engine surfaces by adding utils.brief_text (a cut lands on a boundary, an unclosed bracket group is dropped whole, the elision is marked), but hooks/scripts/orient.py kept its own _clip that returned text[:limit-1] + '…', and handoff_digest still previewed with a raw [:90] slice. The digest's --json view already keeps the untruncated values, so the text panel only had to stop being a second, unaware cutter.
