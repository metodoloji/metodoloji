## Experiment: E-016 — The last decode seam: a non-UTF-8 record is an honest refusal on every record tool, never a traceback

- **Date:** 09.10.2026
- **Status:** completed
- **Theory:** E-009 drew the doctrine and closed one surface: an external file
  that is not valid UTF-8 must end in a machine-readable refusal, because
  `UnicodeDecodeError` is a `ValueError` — neither a `TOMLDecodeError` nor an
  `OSError` — so every handler written as `except (…DecodeError, OSError)` or
  `except OSError` lets it escape as a traceback. E-009 also wrote down, in its
  own Known Gaps, the surface it did NOT probe: "the gate (run_experiment.py)
  and the check scripts". Reading behaviour rather than the promise shows the
  seam is real and wide. Behavior probes with a binary payload (`\xff\xfe\x00…`)
  prove it on every record tool: the gate crashes on `--run` and `--verify`
  (both `open(..., encoding="utf-8")` inside `except OSError`), on `--validate`
  and on `--amend-plan`; `create-qr-record.py` and
  `create-methodology-record.py` crash on `--story`; and `sync-story-qr.py`
  crashes on a corrupt S or QR record. None of those inputs is exotic — a
  record can be written by any editor, a merge, or a damaged disk, exactly the
  provenance E-009 named. The fix follows the per-surface policies E-009 set:
  the gate keeps its "cannot read, rc 2" contract and the record generators
  keep their named-refusal exit 1, while `sync-story-qr.py` — whose job is to
  REPORT drift — gains a `corrupt` verdict that names the file and makes
  `--check` fail, so an unreadable record surfaces instead of silently reading
  as "nothing to sync".
- **Hypothesis:** H-016: "consistency_accuracy >= 0.90"
- **Measurement Metrics:** consistency_accuracy >= 0.90
