## Experiment: E-081 — Imported external context cannot become workflow instructions

- **Date:** 29.10.2026
- **Status:** completed
- **Theory:** E-075 through E-080 established an external-context registry, deterministic discovery, a tested CLI, and conservative version/ref reporting. The research and workflow instructions ask agents to load imported context and upstream documentation, but they do not explicitly say that embedded prompts or commands are untrusted data. Malicious or accidental directives in a source could therefore be mistaken for methodology instructions once the source is brought into the active context.
- **Hypothesis:** H-081: "consistency_accuracy >= 0.90"
- **Measurement Metrics:** consistency_accuracy >= 0.90
- **Experiment Design:** Measure the current consistency bench and its `--broken` seeded-defect control. Add one canonical untrusted-source policy to the methodology, registry guidance, and every external-context-consuming workflow surface. Extend the consistency contract to require the policy on each surface, and seed removal of the policy from a consumer in broken mode. Reproducibility is local and offline.
