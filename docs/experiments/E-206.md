## Experiment: E-206 — The doctor panel counts the orphans and signals its lists withhold

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Status:** completed
- **Record Kind:** contract
- **Theory:** doctor bounds two printed lists: graph orphan endpoints at ten in the report, freshness unclear signals at three in the panel. It then printed what it had as the whole set. Observed live on 2026-11-22 on a scratch board with 14 edges and 6 waiting signals: 'graph 14 edge(s) (10 orphan endpoint(s): ux.ghost00, ux.ghost01, ux.ghost02)' — the report's own cap read as the graph's orphan count, seven of the ten named nowhere — and three of the six unclear rows were simply absent, while the freshness tally went on to claim all six waiting signals. An operator triaging a mis-slugged key or deciding whether to retire a baton reads both lines as the complete set, so a real orphan past the cap and a stale baton past the third never reach the decision. The bounds stay (doctor cannot print an unbounded board), but the report now carries the full totals and the panel names the remainder on both lines — the same rule E-197…E-205 applied to every other bounded list.
